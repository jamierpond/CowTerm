CowTerm shows your terminal sessions on the computers you reach over ssh, and lets you read and type in their panes.

To set it up, open the app and tap Add a computer. Copy the key it shows into ~/.ssh/authorized_keys on that computer, then enter user@computer. The first time it connects, compare the fingerprint it shows with the computer's own host key (ssh-keygen -lf /etc/ssh/ssh_host_ecdsa_key.pub) before you tap Trust.

Each computer needs CowTerm's ct and its session daemon installed. The phone opens your existing sessions there; it cannot start a shell on its own.
