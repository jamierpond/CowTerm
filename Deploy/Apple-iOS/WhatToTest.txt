CowTerm shows the terminal sessions on your computers and lets you read and type in their panes over ssh.

The computer needs CowTerm's ct and its session daemon installed, and the phone must reach it over ssh, for example on Tailscale.

On first launch the app shows "Add a computer". Add the key it shows to ~/.ssh/authorized_keys on that computer: scan the code, or tap Copy key and send it there. Then type the computer as user@computer and tap Connect. The first time, compare the fingerprint the app shows with what the command under it prints on the computer, and tap Trust only if they match.

This beta pairs one computer. To start again, delete and reinstall the app.
